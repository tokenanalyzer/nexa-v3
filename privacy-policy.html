NEXA AI

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

NEXA AI ("we", "our", or "the app") is an AI-powered social media content strategy application. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

NEXA AI is designed with privacy first. We collect minimal data:

  • API Keys: The AI service API keys you enter (Google Gemini, Groq, SambaNova) are stored locally on your device only using AsyncStorage. They are never transmitted to our servers.
  • Generated Content: All content generated by AI and saved to your Vault is stored locally on your device only. We do not store, access, or transmit your content.
  • No Account Required: NEXA AI does not require you to create an account. We do not collect your name, email address, or any personal identifiers.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Your API keys are used solely to make requests to third-party AI services (Google Gemini, Groq, SambaNova) on your behalf.
  • All AI requests are made directly from your device to the respective AI service provider.
  • We do not use your data for advertising, analytics, or any commercial purpose.

3. Third-Party AI Services

When you use NEXA AI, your content prompts are sent to the following third-party AI services based on which API keys you have configured:

  • Google Gemini AI — Google Privacy Policy
  • Groq — Groq Privacy Policy
  • SambaNova Systems — SambaNova Privacy Policy

Please review the privacy policies of these services. We have no control over the data practices of these third-party providers.

4. Data Storage

  • All user data (API keys, saved content, chat history) is stored exclusively on your local device.
  • We do not operate any servers that store personal user data.
  • Uninstalling the app will permanently delete all locally stored data.

5. Data Deletion

You can delete your data at any time:

  • Clear Chat History: Use the "Clear" button in the Intel screen.
  • Delete Vault Items: Swipe or tap delete on any saved item in the Vault screen.
  • Remove API Keys: Clear the key fields in the Keys settings panel.
  • Full Data Deletion: Uninstall the app to remove all data from your device.

6. Children's Privacy

NEXA AI is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

7. Permissions Used

  • INTERNET: Required to communicate with AI service APIs (Gemini, Groq, SambaNova).
  • CLIPBOARD: Used only when you tap a "Copy" button to copy generated content to your clipboard.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the new policy.
